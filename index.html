School library management system

Modern library operations for smarter learning.

SmartLibrary is a proposed school library system designed to make book management, borrowing, returning, and availability tracking easier and more efficient.

Explore SmartLibrary Contact the team

The Problem

Manual library records slow everything down.

Our school's library currently relies on manual methods to manage books and borrowing records.

How the Library Currently Works

The library currently uses physical records to manage books, borrowing and returning activities. This makes it difficult for students and staff to quickly access accurate information about library resources.

  • Students search for books physically in the library.
  • Librarians check physical records to find book information.
  • Borrowing information is written manually in a register.
  • Returned books are updated manually in the records.
  • Finding information about books can take a lot of time.

Who Is Affected?

  • Students – may spend unnecessary time searching for books and checking their availability.
  • Librarians – have to maintain and search through physical records manually.
  • Teachers – may have difficulty quickly finding resources for teaching and research.
  • School Administration – may have difficulty monitoring library resources and maintaining reliable records.

Proposed solution

Our proposed system

SmartLibrary

SmartLibrary is designed to provide an organized digital way to manage books, borrowing, returning, and book availability.

Intended users

  • Librarians
  • Students
  • Lecturers
  • Administrators

SmartLibrary Features

Key features that make managing and using the school library easier.

Feature 1 – Book Management

The librarian can manage the library's books through a simple and organized system.

  • Add new books
  • Edit existing book information
  • Delete books
  • Search for books

Feature 2 – Borrowing and Returning

The system keeps accurate records of books borrowed and returned by students and other users.

  • Student/user who borrowed the book
  • Borrowing date
  • Expected return date
  • Return information

Feature 3 – Book Availability Search

Users can search for a book and quickly check its current availability.

  • Available
  • Already borrowed

Benefits of SmartLibrary

SmartLibrary is designed to provide an organized digital way to manage books, borrowing, returning, and book availability.

  • Saves time
  • Reduces errors
  • Makes books easier to track
  • Makes availability easier to check
  • Reduces dependence on paper records
